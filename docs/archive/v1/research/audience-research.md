# Target Audience Research & Stakeholder Analysis

**Platform:** Hadhramout Center Digital Platform  
**Target Organization:** Hadhramout Center for Historical Studies, Documentation and Publishing  

---

## 1. Audience Segmentation & Needs Analysis

### Persona 1: Dr. Tariq Al-Amoudi (Academic Researcher & University Professor)
- **Profile:** 52 years old, Professor of Modern History at Hadhramout University / King Saud University.
- **Goals:** Needs to cite the latest peer-reviewed studies published in *Majallat Hadramout Al-Thaqafiyyah* and verify conference papers on the Qu'aiti Sultanate.
- **Pain Points:** Current site has no search function, no author filter, no citation generator, and broken routes.
- **Required Solution:** Instant faceted search, author catalog, download options, and one-click citation in APA, Chicago, and MLA formats.

### Persona 2: Sarah Balfagih (Postgraduate Student / Diaspora Scholar)
- **Profile:** 27 years old, Master's student in Middle Eastern Studies in London / Kuala Lumpur.
- **Goals:** Investigating the maritime migration of Hadhramis to the Malay Archipelago and East Africa; needs English abstracts and access to digitized historical monographs.
- **Pain Points:** Most Hadhramout-focused sites are Arabic-only and difficult to navigate on mobile devices.
- **Required Solution:** Bilingual Arabic/English navigation, historical timeline with thematic filters, and clean mobile responsiveness.

### Persona 3: Salem bin Mahfouz (Cultural Enthusiast & Heritage Patron)
- **Profile:** 45 years old, businessman and cultural heritage enthusiast living in Jeddah / Mukalla.
- **Goals:** Wants to stay updated on the Center’s cultural symposia ("منتدى عميد الوفاء"), order newly released books, and watch author lectures ("كاتب وكتاب").
- **Pain Points:** Stale event announcements, broken image links, lack of clear contact channels for book delivery.
- **Required Solution:** Dedicated forum schedule, direct WhatsApp order routing, high-resolution publication covers, and responsive video lecture players.

### Persona 4: Institutional Partners & Foreign Academic Delegations
- **Profile:** Representatives from UNESCO, ALIPH, universities, and regional cultural ministries.
- **Goals:** Assessing the Center’s scientific credentials, institutional governance, past conference proceedings, and collaboration potential.
- **Pain Points:** Broken `/about` link creates an impression of neglect.
- **Required Solution:** Authoritative institutional overview page, verified leadership profiles, documented conference history, and transparent contact channels.
