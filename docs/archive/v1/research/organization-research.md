## 1. Institutional Identity & Founding

The **Hadhramout Center for Historical Studies, Documentation and Publishing** is an independent, non-profit academic research institution established in Mukalla, Hadhramout.

### Core Mission
To investigate, document, publish, and safeguard the historical, civilizational, cultural, and human heritage of Hadhramout, its eminent personalities, its global diaspora, and its interactions with neighboring and worldwide cultures.

### Core Objectives
1. **Scientific Research:** Conduct, support, and supervise rigorous academic research in the history, geography, social structures, and archaeology of Hadhramout.
2. **Manuscript & Document Preservation:** Locate, index, preserve, and publish critical historical documents, manuscripts, letters, treaties, and waqf deeds.
3. **Scholarly Publishing:** Publish peer-reviewed academic journals, historical monographs, translated works, and conference proceedings.
4. **Symposia & Conferences:** Convene regular scientific conferences, seminars, and lectures bringing together historians from Yemen, the Arab world, and international universities.
5. **Cultural Enlightenment:** Deepen public and youth appreciation of Hadhrami identity, ethical values, and civilizational contributions.

## 3. Flagship Programs & Intellectual Assets

### 3.1 Majallat Hadramout Al-Thaqafiyyah (مجلة حضرموت الثقافية)
- A quarterly cultural and historical periodical that has published over 40 substantial volumes.
- Features peer-reviewed studies, unpublished historical letters, interviews with prominent historians, translations of orientalist accounts, book reviews, and creative essays.
- Issue 40 (العدد الأربعون) was published recently and represents a milestone in cultural journalism.

### 3.2 Book Publishing Program (سلسلة إصدارات الكتب)
The Center has published dozens of peer-reviewed historical monographs, including:
1. *Grammatical Contributions of Hadhrami Scholars — Study & Verification* (جهود علماء حضرموت النحوية – دراسة وتحقيق)
2. *Papers on the History of Broom* (أوراق في تاريخ بروم)
3. *Hadhrami History & Historians in the First Half of the 20th Century* (التاريخ والمؤرخون الحضارمة في النصف الأول من القرن العشرين - المؤتمر الخامس)
4. *Ramadan and Eid Eloquent Expressions in Hadhrami Arabic* (ألفاظ رمضانية وعيدية حضرمية فصيحة)
5. *Hadhrami Commerce & Marketing Across Ages: Skill and Identity* (التسويق الحضرمي عبر العصور بين المهارة والهوية)
6. *Hadhramout: From Ingrams Peace to World War II* (حضرموت .. من صلح إنجرامس إلى الحرب العالمية الثانية)
7. *Al-Uqlah: Historical Study via Epigraphic and Archaeological Inscriptions* (العقلة دراسة تاريخية من خلال الشواهد الأثرية والنقشية)
8. *Editorial Articles of Hussein Al-Barr in Al-Ra'id Newspaper* (مقالات حسين البار الافتتاحية بصحيفة الرائد)
9. *Studies in the History of the Qu'aiti Hadhrami Sultanate* (أبحاث في تاريخ السلطنة القعيطية الحضرمية)
10. *Political Relations & Scientific Connections Between Hadhramout and Dhofar in the 7th Century AH* (العلاقات السياسية والصلات العلمية بين حضرموت وظفار في القرن السابع الهجري)
11. *Ports of Ancient Hadhramout (3rd Cent. BCE to 3rd Cent. CE): Sumhuram Port as a Case Study* (موانئ حضرموت - ميناء سمهرم)
12. *Role of Hadhramis in the Construction of the Islamic State in the Early Era & Umayyad Caliphate* (دور الحضارمة في بناء الدولة الإسلامية)

### 3.3 Muntada 'Ameed Al-Wafa Al-Thaqafi (منتدى عميد الوفاء الثقافي)
A premier cultural forum held periodically in Mukalla, featuring academic lectures, literary critiques, and discussions with researchers and intellectuals (e.g., lecture by researcher Khaled Al-Qahoum on *Ishraqat Marsaliyyah*).

### 3.4 Scientific Conferences (المؤتمرات العلمية)
Periodic academic symposia gathering hundreds of research papers on specific eras of Hadhramout's history (such as Qu'aiti Sultanate history, Hadhrami diaspora in the Indian Ocean, South Arabian architecture, and ancient maritime trade).

### 3.5 Media & Video Lectures ("كاتب وكتاب")
Curated video documentation and dialogues with authors and historians discussing their newest contributions and archival discoveries.
