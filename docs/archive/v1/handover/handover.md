# Institutional Handover & Custodianship Agreement

**Parties:**  
1. **Novixa (الطرف الأول - الشركة المنفذة للمبادرة):** Represented by Product Engineering & Strategy Leadership.  
2. **Hadhramout Center for Historical Studies, Documentation and Publishing (الطرف الثاني - المؤسسة المستفيدة):** Mukalla, Hadhramout.  

---

## 1. Nature of the Contribution
This digital platform is engineered and delivered voluntarily by Novixa under the **Novixa Pro Bono Digital Impact Initiative** as a strategic contribution to the preservation and digital dissemination of Hadhramout's historical and cultural heritage.
- **Development Fee:** $0 (Free of development charges).
- **Ownership:** Complete, unencumbered ownership of the source code, digital assets, and platform design is formally transferred to the Hadhramout Center.
- **Intellectual Property:** All historical texts, publication covers, journal articles, and archival photographs remain the exclusive intellectual property of the Hadhramout Center and their respective authors.

---

## 2. Maintenance & Continuity Guarantee
Novixa provides:
1. **Full Source Code Transfer:** Clean, fully commented repository with zero proprietary vendor lock-in.
2. **Hosting Setup:** Initial deployment on global edge infrastructure with zero monthly hosting costs.
3. **Staff Training & Admin Guide:** Comprehensive Arabic-language content management and maintenance documentation.
4. **Warranty & Technical Support:** 12 months of voluntary critical bug fixes and technical advisory by Novixa engineering.
