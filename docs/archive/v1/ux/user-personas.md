# Detailed User Personas & Behavioral Needs

**Platform:** Hadhramout Center Digital Platform  
**Authors:** Novixa UX Strategy Team  

---

### Persona 1: Dr. Tariq Al-Amoudi (Senior University Historian)
* **Age:** 52
* **Role:** Professor of Modern History, Hadhramout University
* **Device:** Desktop (macOS/Chrome, 1440x900) & iPad Safari
* **Primary Motivation:** Finding specific research papers published in *Majallat Hadramout Al-Thaqafiyyah* and citing them in academic publications.
* **Frustrations with current site:** Cannot search by author or keyword; pages are unindexed; missing citation formats.
* **Success Scenario:** Visits `/search` or `/magazines`, types "السلطنة القعيطية", instantly filters to the relevant volume, clicks "نسخ الاقتباس الأكاديمي (APA)" to copy citation directly to bibliography.

---

### Persona 2: Sarah Balfagih (International Postgraduate Researcher)
* **Age:** 27
* **Role:** Master's Candidate in Middle Eastern Studies, London
* **Language:** Fluent English, intermediate academic Arabic
* **Device:** iPhone 15 Pro & MacBook Air
* **Primary Motivation:** Investigating the ancient maritime ports of Hadhramout (Khor Rori / Sumhuram) and the Indian Ocean trade network.
* **Frustrations with current site:** Current site is strictly Arabic-only; no English navigation; layout overflows on mobile screens.
* **Success Scenario:** Lands on the platform, toggles to English, explores the Interactive Timeline (`/timeline`) to trace ancient trade routes, finds the monograph on *Sumhuram Port*, downloads the English abstract.

---

### Persona 3: Omar Ba-Ubaid (Undergraduate Student & Youth Historian)
* **Age:** 21
* **Role:** 3rd-year History Student, College of Arts, Mukalla
* **Device:** Android Smartphone (Samsung A54, 4G connection)
* **Primary Motivation:** Preparing a seminar paper on the peace treaties in Hadhramout (Ingrams treaties); wants to watch recorded lectures by senior historians.
* **Frustrations with current site:** Extremely slow image loading on cellular connections; broken `/about` link; cluttered interface.
* **Success Scenario:** Opens site on mobile, loads instantly via optimized lightweight assets, streams the "كاتب وكتاب" video episode, submits a research inquiry via WhatsApp direct link.
