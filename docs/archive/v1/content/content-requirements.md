# Content Requirements Specification

**Platform:** Hadhramout Center Digital Platform  
**Purpose:** Defining content ingestion, verification rules, and display boundaries  

---

## 1. Required Publication Fields
Every publication catalog entry must satisfy the following minimum requirements before appearing on the public platform:
1. **Title:** Verified Arabic title matching the print edition exactly (`titleAr`), plus standardized English academic translation (`titleEn`).
2. **Author:** Verified author or verification editor name (`authorAr` & `authorEn`).
3. **Category:** Must map to one of the six approved scholarly taxonomies (`linguistics`, `sultanates`, `ports-trade`, `archaeology`, `islamic`, `contemporary`).
4. **Publishing Date:** Gregorian year and Hijri year where documented.
5. **Cover Art:** Valid high-resolution image with fallback to center graphic badge.
6. **Abstract:** 2-3 paragraph verified academic summary of book contents and findings.
7. **Bibliographic Citations:** Pre-computed APA 7th, Chicago 17th, and MLA 9th citations.

---

## 2. Magazine Issues Specification
1. **Issue Number:** Sequential numbering verified against the physical publication archive.
2. **Publication Season:** Season and year in Arabic and English.
3. **Featured Article List:** Titles and authors of key studies included in that volume.
4. **Physical Metadata:** Page count, article count, and publisher imprint.
