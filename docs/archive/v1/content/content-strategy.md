# Content Strategy & Editorial Principles

**Platform:** Hadhramout Center Digital Platform  
**Guideline:** Absolute Factual Integrity & Academic Precision  

---

## 1. Editorial Philosophy
Historical scholarship requires scrupulous fidelity to documented records. Novixa enforces a strict **Zero-Fabrication Policy**:
* **No Artificial Leadership:** All board members, presidents, and editors must be verified from center publications or marked `UNKNOWN — REQUIRES CONFIRMATION`.
* **No Invented Publications:** Every book, paper, and magazine volume indexed must map to actual print editions issued by the Center.
* **No Inflated Statistics:** Claims of "thousands of manuscripts" or "millions of readers" are strictly forbidden unless backed by official documentation.

---

## 2. Bilingual Editorial Voice
* **Arabic Tone:** Dignified, classical academic Arabic (لغة أكاديمية رصينة، معجمية دقيقة، خالية من التكلف الإنشائي، محتفية بالتراث الحضرمي).
* **English Tone:** Precise, objective, scholarly English conforming to international Middle Eastern and South Arabian historical scholarship conventions.

---

## 3. Content Freshness & Lifecycle
* **Active Announcements:** Forthcoming lectures for *Muntada 'Ameed Al-Wafa* and calls for conference papers prominently badged.
* **Archival Immobility:** Historical monograph metadata and past conference proceedings are treated as permanent immutable records with persistent citation references.
