# Candidate Evaluation & Decision Matrix

**Initiative:** Novixa Pro Bono Digital Impact  
**Scope:** Evaluation of 22 Regional Institutions across Hadhramout (Mukalla, Seiyun, Tarim, Shibam, Ghayl Ba Wazir, Ash Shihr)  
**Author:** Novixa Strategy & Architecture Team  
**Status:** Approved  

---

## 1. Evaluation Methodology

Each candidate was audited across **6 Core Strategic Dimensions** (scored 1 to 5, total weighted score out of 100):

1. **Institutional Impact & Cultural Value (Weight: 20%)**: Societal stature, depth of contribution to Hadhramout and Arab/Islamic heritage.
2. **Digital Need & Technical Urgency (Weight: 20%)**: Deficiencies in current digital presence (broken links, lack of search, mobile issues, 404 errors, lack of modern IA).
3. **Content Richness & Authenticity (Weight: 20%)**: Availability of verifiable primary content (books, periodicals, conference papers, historical archives, multimedia).
4. **Target Audience Diversity & Academic Reach (Weight: 15%)**: Global/regional scholars, students, diaspora, cultural bodies, public interest.
5. **Feasibility & Operational Autonomy (Weight: 15%)**: Realism of autonomous completion, maintainability without forcing heavy ops overhead.
6. **Novixa Strategic Case Study Value (Weight: 10%)**: Showcase value for product engineering, multilingual RTL/LTR architecture, search systems, and institutional UX.

## 4. Final Recommendation: Hadhramout Center for Historical Studies, Documentation and Publishing

|----------------------|--------|---------------------------------------|-----------------------------------|------------------------------------------|

| Feasibility & Autonomy| 15% | 5 (100% - independent scientific body) | 3.5 (70% - grant bureaucracy) | 4 (80%) |

### 4.2 The Winning Rationales
1. **Verifiable Intellectual Treasury:** The Center has published over 40 issues of *Majallat Hadramout Al-Thaqafiyyah*, numerous scholarly volumes (Qu'aiti history, Ingrams treaties, ancient ports of Sumhuram/Khor Rori, Islamic expansion, linguistic studies), and regular academic symposia ("منتدى عميد الوفاء").
2. **Acute Digital Need:** The existing Next.js deployment at `hadramout.center` is broken:
   - Clicking `/about` produces an unhandled 404 error ("الصفحة غير موجودة").
   - There is NO search capability across the entire site.
   - Missing author taxonomies, subject categorizations, and academic citation tools.
   - Zero English support, severely cutting off foreign researchers, orientalists, and second/third-generation Hadhrami diaspora in Southeast Asia and the Gulf.
   - Broken image thumbnails (`Update` placeholder) and poor mobile typography.
3. **Strategic Alignment with Novixa:** Demonstrating how Novixa can build a **State-of-the-Art Institutional Knowledge & Archival Platform** establishes Novixa as a premier product engineering firm capable of serving universities, ministries, and international research organizations.
