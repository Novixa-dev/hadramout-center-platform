# Product Definition — Hadhramout Digital Knowledge & Archival Platform

**Platform Name:** منصة مركز حضرموت للدراسات التاريخية الرقمية  
**English Name:** Hadhramout Historical Research & Digital Archival Platform  
**Target Organization:** Hadhramout Center for Historical Studies, Documentation and Publishing (مركز حضرموت للدراسات التاريخية والتوثيق والنشر)  
**Parent Initiative:** Novixa Pro Bono Digital Impact  
**Architect:** Novixa Product Engineering  

---

## 1. Product Category
**"Institutional Knowledge & Archival Research Platform"** (منصة معرفية وأرشيفية أكاديمية متكاملة)

This is NOT a generic marketing brochure or simple static blog. It is an **interactive digital repository and academic communications engine** tailored for historians, university researchers, cultural enthusiasts, students, and the worldwide Hadhrami diaspora.

---

## 2. Product Vision
To be the authoritative digital gateway to the history, documentation, cultural heritage, and intellectual output of Hadhramout—providing instant, beautifully presented, bilingual access to peer-reviewed periodicals, scholarly books, conference proceedings, historical archives, and academic forums.

## 4. Key Value Propositions by Audience
* **For Academic Researchers & University Professors:** Fast multi-attribute search, verified bibliographic citations (APA, MLA, Chicago), full issue contents, and direct access to conference studies.
* **For the International Hadhrami Diaspora:** High-fidelity bilingual interface (Arabic/English), rich historical timelines (ancient ports, Qu'aiti & Kathiri eras, diaspora migrations to Southeast Asia & East Africa), and cultural articles.
* **For Students & General Public:** Mobile-first, lightweight reader experience, video lectures ("كاتب وكتاب"), downloadable publications, and direct channels for academic consultation.
* **For Center Administration:** Zero-friction operational structure, transparent content schemas, automated SEO metadata, and zero hosting debt.
