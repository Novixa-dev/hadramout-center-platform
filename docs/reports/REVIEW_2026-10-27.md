## 2. Social preview (OG) — every page type

Cards are generated server-side (sharp + Pango, so Arabic is shaped correctly), 1200×630 PNG, with the Center's logo, name, a type label, the title, author/date and the host. Their URLs carry the design revision (`?d=4`) and the document's `updatedAt`, so the 30-day edge cache never serves an outdated card.

| Page | og:type | Image | Extra tags |
|---|---|---|---|
| Book | `book` | **real cover** (framed 2:3) + title + author + year | `book:author`, `book:isbn` when known, `book:release_date` only with a day-precise date |
| Magazine issue | `website` | real cover + title + issue/date (issue no longer repeated when the title has it) | Scholar `citation_*` |
| News | `article` | the news photo full-bleed, title on a shaded band | `article:published_time` (only true dates), `article:modified_time`, section |
| Article | `article` | **new:** the article's first card-worthy illustration (of its first three images), framed beside the title, for about 42% of articles (sample of 114 of 909). The other articles' images are author thumbnails under 480 px or title strips, which would look blurry, so they keep the branded text card | `article:author` URLs, section, Scholar meta |
| Video | `video.other` when a YouTube id exists (thumbnail on the card) | `og:video` embed | video 2 has no video in the source data — editorial item |
| Event / forum | `website` | branded text card (the source has no event photos: 0/64) | Event JSON-LD only with a start date |
| Author | **`profile`** (was `website`) | name + number of works | ProfilePage JSON-LD |
| Lists, home, topics, timeline, search, saved, contact | `website` | branded page card with its lead line | CollectionPage / WebSite JSON-LD |
| About / privacy / terms | `website` | page card with its own title | — |

**Decision recorded:** a book's `og:image` is a composed card with the cover, not the bare cover file. Covers are portrait (2:3); Facebook, WhatsApp, X and LinkedIn crop `summary_large_image` previews to about 1.91:1, which would cut a bare cover to a strip through its middle. The card shows the whole cover sharply, plus the title and the Center's name. The bare cover stays the `image` in the Book JSON-LD for search engines.

Article JSON-LD now always has an `image`: the cover, or the card, which is 1200×630 and meets Google's Article image guidance.

## 5. Data issues found (editorial, not code)

- Category 3 «تحقيق وتصوير: عبدالله عوض بكير - صالح الفردي» is a byline, not a topic, left by the legacy import. It shows as the type label on article 4's share card. Move the names into the article's authors and delete the category (an admin action).
- Video 2 has no YouTube id or URL: the page has nothing to play. Add the link or unpublish it.
- 0 of 64 events and forum sessions have a photo. One photo per event would make their share cards far richer.
- About 58% of articles have no illustration that works on a card: 88 have no image at all, the others only small thumbnails or title strips. Today the only way to improve one is to put a large photo (≥ 1200 px wide) among the first three images of its body. Proposal (NEXT_PLAN): give articles an optional "lead image" field, which the card and JSON-LD would use first.
